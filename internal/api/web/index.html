    Add Job

    Paste a job link from TopCV, VietnamWorks, JobOKO, CareerLink or Facebook, or paste the job description text. For a Facebook post, paste the link and then the post text under it. braveduck doesn't open Facebook.

    ✨

    The system will parse details automatically (company, role, salary, skills, active date, distance) and score the job using AI models.

    Your profile
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Every job is rated against this. Fill in what applies to you, in any language: what you're looking for,
          your minimum pay, where you can work, and your deal-breakers. Lines inside <!-- --> are hints and are ignored.
        
    
        
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    My CV
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Upload your CV once: a PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown (.md) or HTML file, up to 5 MB.
          braveduck keeps the file on this computer to use when you apply, and copies its text into the
          About me part of your profile, which jobs are rated and CVs are tailored against.
